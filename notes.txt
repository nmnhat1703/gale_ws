  For a first hover-oscillation tuning pass, try:

  ref_cost = 1e2      # was 1e3
  vel_cost = 1e2      # was 1e1
  ang_cost = 1e1

  ref_cost_e = 1e2    # was 1e3
  vel_cost_e = 1e2    # was 1e1
  ang_cost_e = 1e1

  That makes the controller less aggressive about instantly killing position error, and
  more aggressive about damping velocity. In practice, that usually reduces hover
  hunting.

  Then regenerate the acados solver, because these weights are baked into the generated
  solver:

  cd ~/gale_ws
